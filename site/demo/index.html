Teach Kopi to move a client enquiry into a simple lead inbox. Then try a new enquiry with a completely different layout.

1. Open enquiry A ↗2. Open lead inbox ↗3. Open enquiry B ↗

Rehearse this once before presenting

  1. Keep Kopi and its paired extension running. Start the local model before presenting.
  2. Open enquiry A and the inbox in two tabs. On enquiry A, click Start observing in Kopi.
  3. Copy the contact name, email, project and budget from enquiry A into the matching inbox fields. Use ordinary select-text → Ctrl+C → Ctrl+V. Click Save lead and wait until the green Lead saved confirmation appears.
  4. Return to Kopi and choose Stop & discover. Review the proposed inputs, destination, field mappings and success check. Answer genuine questions, then approve and save.
  5. Switch to enquiry B. In Kopi, select the saved workflow and Preview from current tab. The values should be Sam's, not Maya's.
  6. Approve the run. Return to the inbox and show that Sam was added while Maya remains.

This is a demo website for generic browser actions, not a built-in “lead inbox” workflow. Kopi must learn the mapping from your demonstration. The model can ask questions; do not skip the review.

90-second presentation: “I show it how I work once. It learns the meaning of the fields, not just a sequence of clicks. This second enquiry uses a different layout. I review the newly extracted details, approve, and the inbox updates. The model runs locally, and I stay signed in to my normal browser.”

Saved leads stay in this browser's local storage for this site. No emails, messages, payments or external form submissions are sent. Clear the demo rows in the inbox before rehearsing again. Kopi's duplicate-prevention receipts are separate; use a new saved workflow for a fresh rehearsal if the app blocks the same source URL.

Demo fixtures only. Browser-page behaviour is testable independently; model learning and extension execution must be rehearsed in your actual paired browser.
