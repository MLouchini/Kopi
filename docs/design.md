# Kopi visual direction

The final website uses generous ivory space, large Manrope typography, orange marker highlights and the exact logo supplied by the user. A logo tile sits inside the headline. The app has a simpler white workspace, compact top navigation and direct recording/review controls; marketing headings and decorative hero artwork were removed as requested.

Core colours: ivory, ink #19191E, orange #FF7A1A and pale orange selection surfaces. Keep decoration away from app forms and execution controls. Brand assets are site/assets/kopi-logo.png and kopi-wordmark.png; WPF embeds the same supplied assets.

An exploratory transparent loop was generated with the built-in imagegen tool and saved as site/assets/kopi-loop.png. It is retained as an exploration and is not used as the final logo. Its prompt follows.

## Illustration prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a premium 3D brand illustration for Kopi, a friendly local desktop workflow assistant, to share between a cream editorial landing page and a desktop app. Create ONE sculptural continuous looping ribbon shaped like a softly rounded, slightly asymmetric infinity loop / folded circular knot, photographed as a tangible beautiful object. Translucent frosted resin and opalescent glass with fine tactile grain, polished soft rounded edges, beautiful visible thickness, peach orange flowing through pale golden yellow and pink into lavender lilac. Sophisticated natural studio lighting from upper left, subtle internal refractions and silky specular highlights. Three-quarter front view with a gentle 15 degree tilt, centered, object fills 80% of square image. Strong readable silhouette even at small size. Actually transparent background with clean alpha, no ground plane, no background rectangle, no text, no lettering, no watermark, no logos, no interface, no other objects. Understated contemporary editorial art direction, tactile rather than neon sci-fi. The references for the surrounding visual direction are warm ivory paper, dark ink typography, playful pastel sticker accents and airy high-end product design; those are context only, do not draw a website.

## Final hero wave

Asset: site/assets/hero-wave.png. Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, with a transparent background. The supplied Kopi logo remains unchanged. The decorative wave sits above the heading; the hero layout must keep its download button visible within the viewport.

Prompt: Create a very wide, shallow decorative hero illustration for Kopi, a local browser workflow assistant. Aspect ratio about 3:1. A single elegant flowing orange and apricot ribbon wave moves gently from left to right, like a softly sculpted stream of paper and translucent satin. Nestled naturally along the ribbon are a few beautifully crafted small illustrations relevant to browser automation: a cream browser window with blank lines, a tiny form card with orange checkmarks, a rounded cursor arrow, two little documents and a small sun-like sparkle. Sophisticated editorial 3D/clay/paper illustration, warm tangerine orange, pale peach, soft golden yellow, cream; very restrained near-black details. Soft natural studio lighting, tactile materials, subtle shadows. Airy, clean, premium and playful. Keep the objects small and widely spaced, preserve plenty of transparent negative space; the wave is a decorative header band, not a large busy scene. No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks. Actual transparent background with clean alpha. The illustration will sit on warm off-white above a very large black typographic headline; keep the entire composition within a shallow horizontal band.
